All four apps

These apps have no accounts and no servers of their own. Nothing you put in them is sent to me. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

On your phone

Everything you add stays in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Google Drive save (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive in Settings, you sign in with your Google account and the app saves a copy of its data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so a new phone can bring it back. The app asks only for that hidden folder (it cannot see your other Drive files) and your email address, which it shows so you know which account is used. I never see the copy or your email.

To stop: turn Google Drive off in the app's Settings. To delete the copy: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings, then Manage apps, find the app and choose Delete hidden app data.

Tips

Tips in Support the developer are paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; I do not get your card or bank details. The app remembers on your phone that you tipped, so it stops asking for a while.

Children

The apps are not made for children under 13 and do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Better Deal

  • Camera: used only when you snap a price tag. The tag is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit text recognition; the photo is not uploaded anywhere. ML Kit may send Google anonymous figures about how well it runs, never your photos.
  • Your tags and photos stay on your phone, and in your Google Drive if you turn that on.

Stocks

  • What you type (what you own, buys, sells and dividends) stays on your phone, and in your Google Drive if you turn that on.
  • Prices: to show current prices, the app asks public market sites for the symbols you hold: Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq, Settrade and Bitkub. Exchange rates come from open.er-api.com and Frankfurter. Like any web request, these sites see the symbol asked for and your internet address. How much you own is never sent.
  • Notifications: reminders about your pending orders, made on the phone.

Vacation

  • Your trips (bags, items, places, notes, photos and voice notes) stay on your phone, and in your Google Drive if you turn that on.
  • Location: only when you ask for where you are. The point is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim to name the place, and to an OpenStreetMap Overpass server (overpass-api.de, or its mirror at maps.mail.ru) to find places nearby. The app does not track you in the background.
  • Maps: map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, Esri and Stadia Maps, which see the area you look at and your internet address.
  • Calendar: read on your phone to find your flights. Nothing from it is sent anywhere.
  • Camera and microphone: only for the photos and voice notes you choose to add.
  • Links to Google Maps or Google search open in your browser or the Maps app, under Google's own policy.
  • Notifications: reminders such as check-out, made on the phone.

Planner

  • Your events and to-dos stay on your phone, and in your Google Drive if you turn that on.
  • Calendar: read on your phone to show your calendars' events in the app and the widget. Nothing from it is sent anywhere.
  • Alarms and notifications: the reminders you set, made on the phone.
